# Company Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to Contoso Clinical Research. This handbook provides general information about company policies, workplace expectations, and employee resources.

## Business Hours

* Monday through Friday
* 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

## Employee Benefits

Employees are eligible for:

* Health insurance
* Dental insurance
* Paid time off
* Professional development opportunities
* Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

## Workplace Expectations

Employees are expected to:

* Follow company security policies.
* Protect confidential information.
* Complete annual security awareness training.
* Report suspected security incidents to the Information Security team.

## Contact Information

For general questions, contact Human Resources at [hr@contosoclinicalresearch.com](mailto:hr@contosoclinicalresearch.com).
