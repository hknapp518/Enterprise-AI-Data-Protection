# Clinical Trial Protocol

## Document Information

* **Protocol ID:** CTR-2026-014
* **Study Name:** CardioNova Phase II Clinical Trial
* **Document Owner:** Clinical Research Department
* **Classification:** Confidential – Clinical
* **Authorized Groups:** Clinical Users, Clinical Data Team, Research Leads

## Study Overview

This clinical trial evaluates the safety and effectiveness of CardioNova, an investigational treatment for adults with moderate cardiovascular disease.

The study will enroll approximately 250 participants across multiple clinical research sites. Participants will be monitored for treatment response, adverse events, and changes in cardiovascular health over a twelve-month period.

## Eligibility Criteria

Participants must meet the following requirements:

* Be between 40 and 75 years old
* Have a confirmed diagnosis of moderate cardiovascular disease
* Provide written informed consent
* Meet the study’s clinical screening requirements

## Restricted Information

This document contains confidential clinical research information and is intended only for authorized clinical and research personnel.

External partners and unauthorized business users must not receive access to the full protocol.
